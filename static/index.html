03 / NOTES
PJ_07 · PROJECT OVERVIEW

Bennett University

Multimodal NLP for Deepfake Detection and Explainable Content Verification

VeriFuse is a runnable research prototype for comparing text and image signals, testing fusion strategies, and making model decisions easier to inspect.

The included dataset is generated locally from simple colored shapes and contains controlled visual changes and caption inconsistencies. Its purpose is to support reproducible experiments and demonstrate the full workflow.

Text + visionFusion comparisonExplainabilityOOD evaluation

Read the result with care.

A REAL or FAKE label describes the model’s decision within this synthetic benchmark. It is not a factual verdict about an image, a person, or an event.

  • Unfamiliar uploads may be outside the training distribution and return UNCERTAIN.
  • Highlighted words and regions show model sensitivity, not proof of manipulation.
  • Confidence and probability are model scores; they are not guarantees of correctness.
  • Real-world use needs independently sourced data, stronger models, and external validation.
